## Continuity protocol

1. Identify conflict.
2. Classify: canon / research / visual / production.
3. Do not silently rewrite.
4. Add Decision Log entry.
5. Assess affected episodes.
6. Approve change.
7. Update this ledger.
8. Re-run QC.
