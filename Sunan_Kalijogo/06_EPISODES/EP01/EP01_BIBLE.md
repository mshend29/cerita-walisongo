# EP01 — ANAK ADIPATI

**Status:** production draft / source [T]+[D]  
**Duration:** 75–90 sec  
**Format:** 9:16  
**Theme:** Niat baik  
**Emotion:** MARAH → BERANI

## Canon

Raden Said sees rakyat suffering and chooses to take food from the kadipaten warehouse for them. The act is intentionally framed as morally conflicted: the intention is helping, but the action is still theft.

Sari and Jaka are fictional composite characters [D].

## Screenplay draft

1. Jaka, holding an empty bowl: “Bu... masih ada?”
2. Raden Said sees hungry rakyat and a full warehouse: “Kalau makanan itu ada... mengapa mereka tetap lapar?”
3. “Aku tidak bisa hanya melihat.”
4. At night, a guard passes the warehouse. Raden hides.
5. He opens the warehouse and takes one food sack: “Kalau ini salah... biar aku yang menanggungnya.”
6. He gives food to Sari. “Raden... ini dari mana?” Jaka smiles.
7. The guard discovers the open warehouse and footprints.
8. Raden turns. Narration: “Niatnya adalah menolong. Tapi malam itu... ia telah memilih jalan yang salah.”

**End hook:** EP02 — Nama yang Diburu.
