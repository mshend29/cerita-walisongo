# VISUAL STYLE BIBLE — SUNAN KALIJOGO

## Master style

**Cinematic Javanese Historical Drama**

### Global visual direction

- realistic late-15th / early-16th-century Java
- grounded human faces
- natural materials
- earthy muted palette
- natural lighting
- cinematic depth
- vertical 9:16
- facial emotion prioritized
- spiritual moments expressed through environment and restraint rather than fantasy magic

### Framing priority

1. extreme close-up
2. close-up
3. medium
4. over-the-shoulder
5. wide establishing

- guns

- Chinese imperial styling
- neon

- plastic/concrete urban look
- text/watermark/logo
- duplicate people
