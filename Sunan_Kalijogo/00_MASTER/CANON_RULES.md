# CANON RULES — SUNAN KALIJOGO

## Source hierarchy

1. Current locked Story Bible.
2. Approved Research Ledger.
3. Locked Decision Log.
4. Continuity Ledger.
5. Character / Location / Prop / Visual Bibles.
6. Episode documents.
7. Chat discussion and ideas — **not canon until recorded**.

## Source-status labels

- [H] Historis
- [T] Tradisi
- [L] Legenda
- [D] Dramatisasi

A claim may carry multiple labels.

## Canon change protocol

**IDE → SOURCE → STATUS → TIMELINE IMPACT → CHARACTER IMPACT → VISUAL IMPACT → APPROVAL → CANON**

If there is no reliable source but the idea is useful for drama, label it [D].

## Non-negotiable

- Do not turn legend into fact.
- Do not add exact dates without support.
- Do not change character motivation for one episode.
- Do not create unexplained jumps in age, location, relationships, or important props.
- Every episode must create a changed state or forward question.
- Every episode must check previous state, current change, and future consequence.
